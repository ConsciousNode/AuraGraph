# AuraGraph reading, packet 2

You'll be shown 6 images. Each is a picture of a short sound clip (an "AuraGraph tile").

How to read a tile:
- Time runs left to right (ticks in seconds along the bottom). Pitch runs bottom to top on a log scale (each C is labelled with its frequency in Hz on the left).
- The caption under each image says how loudness is drawn in that image. The drawing style differs from image to image; read each caption.
- Horizontal lines are sustained pitched sounds. Evenly spaced parallel lines above a lowest line are that sound's overtones (its timbre). Curved or slanted lines are pitch slides.
- Vertical stripes are hits (drums, clicks, strikes). Sounds that ring out fade toward the right.
- Low rows are bass; the top rows are very high (cymbals, hiss).

For each tile, answer the questions. Give numbers (times in seconds, notes or Hz) wherever you can. If you can't tell, or something isn't there, say so plainly: "not there" and "can't tell" are good answers when they're true. End each tile with a confidence from 1 (guess) to 5 (sure).


## Tile 1 (`tile-1.png`)

1. At what time does everything go silent in the middle, and for how long?
2. List every sound you can find, including the quietest. For each, give its approximate frequency range and its start and end times.
3. Confidence, 1-5.

## Tile 2 (`tile-2.png`)

1. Describe how the pitch moves over time: where it slides smoothly, where it holds, where it moves in separate steps, and where nothing sounds. Give the notes (or Hz) and times at each change.
2. Which chord is playing underneath?
3. Confidence, 1-5.

## Tile 3 (`tile-3.png`)

1. How many separate events are there, and when does each start?
2. For each event, which notes are sounding (note names or approximate Hz)? If any are chords, say whether each sounds major or minor.
3. At what time does the singer come in?
4. Confidence, 1-5.

## Tile 4 (`tile-4.png`)

1. How many parts are there, and when does each start? Are they at the same pitch or different pitches? Give the note or Hz for each.
2. When does the drum come in?
3. For each part, describe its pattern of overtones: how many lines above the lowest one, whether they are evenly spaced, and whether the pattern changes over time.
4. Confidence, 1-5.

## Tile 5 (`tile-5.png`)

1. Are there pitched (tonal) sounds, unpitched sounds, or both?
2. List each kind of sound event you can tell apart, how many times each occurs, and when. Estimate the tempo if there is one.
3. What key is the melody in?
4. Confidence, 1-5.

## Tile 6 (`tile-6.png`)

1. Is there a melody? If so, list its notes in order: note name (or approximate Hz) and start time for each.
2. Is anything sounding underneath or below it? If so, what, and when?
3. What rhythm do the drums play?
4. Confidence, 1-5.
