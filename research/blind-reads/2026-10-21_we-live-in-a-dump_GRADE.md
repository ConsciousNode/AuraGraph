## Lessons
1. **Priors are per song, not per album.** The "accordion right" layout from two songs made me expect an accordion that wasn't there. Use a prior to know where to look, never as evidence.
2. **Struck vs held:** the intro stabs and the ending were straight stacks that **start bright and decay**: the Gymnopédie piano look. I wrote "chord stabs" and "keys or accordion" without applying the decay test. Rule: straight + decaying = struck (piano); straight + holding = reed/organ.
3. **A high sung "ayyy" vs a whistle:** measured both. The "ayyy" (~974 Hz) and the 1:42 whistle (~958 Hz) both had the 2nd harmonic ~14-15 dB below the 1st in this mix; the whistle wandered more (pitch spread 166 vs 50 cents). In a crowd mix at ~1 kHz they look almost the same; context (a scream right before it, an audience moment) is the better clue. Logged as an honest limit, not a rule.
4. A Wilhelm scream compressed into ~0.2 s looks like a "chirp". Short glide events near speech can be vocal stunts.
5. Breakdown boundary 2 s late: I took the start from where the mids are fully gone; the music starts thinning earlier. Next time report "thins from X, bare from Y".
