AURAGRAPH

Sound for LLMs: audio turned into pictures a language model can read. And, it turns out, pictures that can be played back.

Frame-grid lets a model "watch" a video as numbered grids of frames. AuraGraph is its sibling for sound: audio becomes tiles, image panels plus a short text sidecar, that any model with image input can read. Left to right is time, bottom to top is pitch, brightness is loudness. There are no audio tokenizers for these models, so sound has to arrive as something they can see and count.

What we've found so far

  • Models can read tiles. Blind reads of real songs, graded against human ears, found voices, hits, ladders of overtones, bass lines, tempo and structure. FINDINGS · blind reads · outside test
  • A tile can be played back. From the picture alone, 94% of the melody returns, frame for frame. It sounds like a cover, not the recording. INVERSION-1
  • Melody lives on the pitch axis, rhythm on the time axis. Coarsen one and the other survives. What makes a tile readable as music is exactly what makes it playable.
  • Which drawing do models read best? Plain gray, with the grid burned in: 95% for the models that can read tiles, against 83% for the old colour map. Readers fool themselves the same way whatever the drawing: they bend a real mark to fit the question. LEGIBILITY-1
  • Where it could go: a model that sings by drawing, a diffusion model that paints music, music as a PNG. SPEC-SEEDS

Developed in public
