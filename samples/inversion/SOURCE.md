# Inversion examples

All four files are 15 s (the sweep ones: 8–15 s) from **"Rakes of Kildare"** (traditional Irish tune) played on a Clavietta melodica by **Troy Banarzi**, uploaded to Wikimedia Commons by user *Melodicas*: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Melodica_rakesofkildare.ogg. Dual-licensed GFDL 1.2+ / **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

- `melodica_original_15s.mp3`: the recording itself (trimmed, re-encoded)
- `melodica_rebuilt_160bands_1000px.mp3`: rebuilt from an AuraGraph-resolution tile only
- `melodica_24bands_fulltime.mp3`: rebuilt from a 24-band tile (the tune melts, the rhythm stays)
- `melodica_160bands_100px.mp3`: rebuilt from a 100-column tile (the rhythm smears, the tune stays)

The rebuilds are derivatives of the recording, so they're shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0** too. See `research/INVERSION-1.md`.
